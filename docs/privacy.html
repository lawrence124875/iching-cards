謙卦 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日
開發者：LC Lab（Lawrence Chang）　聯絡信箱：lawrence124875@gmail.com

「謙卦」（以下稱「本 App」）是以《易經》六十四卦為題材的自我探索與反思工具。本 App 不需要註冊或登入，解讀內容全部預先寫在 App 裡，不使用 AI 服務，也不會把您寫下的文字傳到任何伺服器。本政策說明本 App 會蒐集哪些資訊、如何使用，以及您擁有的權利。

只存在您手機裡的資料

  • 卦記：您保存的卦、寫下的「想問的事」、事後的回顧筆記，以及回顧提醒的時間，只儲存在您的裝置本機。開發者看不到，App 也不會自動上傳、備份或分享。您可以在卦記列表右上角選「備份卦記」，自行把備份檔存到您選擇的地方（例如您的雲端硬碟），日後用「從備份還原」取回；備份檔不會經過開發者。解除安裝本 App 或清除 App 資料而沒有備份時，這些內容會一併刪除且無法復原。
  • 設定與次數：例如呼吸音景的時長、鈴聲與雙耳節拍開關，以及免費版當日已起卦的次數，同樣只存在本機。

為改善 App 而蒐集的資訊

  • 匿名使用統計（Firebase Analytics）：記錄少數操作事件，以了解功能是否好用，例如：顯示了一次解讀（起卦方式、卦序 1–64、是否有變爻）、保存了一則卦記（是否設定提醒）、開始或完成一次呼吸練習（卦序、分鐘數、是否開啟無聲引導或雙耳節拍）。Firebase Analytics 也會自動收集一般的裝置與使用資訊（例如裝置型號、系統版本、App 版本、大致地區、使用時段），並使用 App 實例識別碼等裝置識別碼。
    統計絕不包含您寫下的「想問的事」、回顧筆記或任何您輸入的文字。
  • 當機報告（Firebase Crashlytics）：App 發生當機時，蒐集錯誤堆疊、裝置型號、系統版本等診斷資訊，用於修正問題，不包含您的個人身分資訊或卦記內容。
  • 功能設定（Firebase Remote Config）：App 會定期下載功能開關設定（例如暫時關閉某項功能），此過程會使用 App 實例識別碼，不傳送您的個人資料。

廣告（免費版）

免費版會在首頁與卦記列表底部顯示橫幅廣告、看完解讀回到起卦頁時偶爾顯示插頁廣告，並提供您自行選擇觀看的獎勵廣告（看完可多起一卦，或在卦記達免費上限時多存一則）。呼吸練習與閱讀解讀時不會跳出廣告。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）可能蒐集下列資料：

  • 裝置或其他 ID：Android 廣告 ID（AAID）、App 組識別碼等。
  • 大略位置：由 IP 位址推得的國家或城市層級位置；本 App 不使用 GPS，也不取得精確位置。
  • 應用程式互動：廣告的曝光、點擊與觀看紀錄，以及 App 啟動等使用資訊。
  • 當機記錄與診斷資料：廣告 SDK 本身的錯誤與效能資訊（例如載入時間、延遲）。
  • 裝置與 App 資訊：裝置型號、系統版本、語言、App 版本。

這些資料用於廣告或行銷（投放廣告、限制頻率；在您同意時顯示個人化廣告）、數據分析（計算廣告成效）以及詐欺防範、安全性與法規遵循（偵測無效流量與廣告詐欺）。開發者只看得到 AdMob 的彙總報表，無法辨識個別使用者。

  • 在需要取得同意的地區（例如歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士），App 會先以 Google 的同意表單詢問您，之後可從首頁右上角選單的「廣告隱私設定」隨時更改。
  • 您可在裝置系統設定中重設或刪除廣告識別碼，或選擇停用個人化廣告。
  • 本 App 在 AdMob 封鎖酒精、賭博等敏感類別的廣告，廣告內容分級上限為「家長指導（PG）」。
  • 訂閱會員不顯示任何廣告，也不會載入廣告。

AdMob 的資料處理方式請見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

訂閱（謙卦會員）

月訂與年訂經由 Google Play 結帳完成，付款資料（信用卡等）由 Google 處理，開發者看不到。訂閱狀態透過 RevenueCat 服務確認：RevenueCat 會收到一組由 App 隨機產生的匿名使用者 ID（格式如 $RCAnonymousID:…，與您的 Google 帳戶、姓名或電子郵件無關）、購買記錄（方案、價格與幣別、購買與到期時間、Google Play 訂單編號與購買憑證）以及裝置與 App 基本資訊，只用於確認您是否為會員、恢復購買與訂閱統計，不含您的姓名或電子郵件。訂閱會自動續訂，可隨時在 Google Play 的「付款和訂閱」中取消，取消後會員資格保留到本期結束。RevenueCat 的資料處理方式請見 RevenueCat 隱私權政策。

App 使用的權限

  • 通知、準時鬧鐘、開機後重新排程：只用於您自行設定的「卦記回顧提醒」，在您選定的日子提醒您回來看這一卦。
  • 前景服務（媒體播放）、保持喚醒：只用於呼吸音景，讓您關閉螢幕或切到其他 App 時音景能繼續播放，並在鎖定畫面顯示暫停、停止鍵。
  • 網路、廣告識別碼：用於上述 Firebase 服務、顯示廣告（免費版）與確認訂閱狀態。
  • Google Play 結帳：只用於購買與恢復訂閱。

本 App 不會做的事

  • 不要求註冊帳號或登入。
  • 不蒐集姓名、電話、電子郵件、地址、相片、聯絡人或精確位置。
  • 不出售或出租任何使用者資料。
  • 不使用 AI 服務分析您的提問或筆記。

資料分享

上述統計、當機與設定資料由 Google 的 Firebase 服務處理；廣告由 Google AdMob 處理；訂閱由 Google Play 與 RevenueCat 處理（見上方各段）。傳輸過程皆經過加密。除此之外，本 App 不與任何第三方分享資料，也絕不分享您的卦記與輸入的文字。Firebase 的資料處理方式請見 Firebase 隱私權與安全性說明。

兒童隱私

本 App 並非為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意向兒童蒐集個人資訊。

內容性質

本 App 的卦象解讀僅供自我探索與娛樂參考，不構成任何醫療、心理、法律、財務或其他專業建議，也不保證預測結果。

刪除資料

本段適用於 Android App 「謙卦」（套件名稱 com.lclab.qiangua），開發者 LC Lab。本 App 不需要帳號，因此沒有「刪除帳號」的步驟；各類資料的刪除方式如下：

  • 卦記、想問的事、回顧筆記、設定與次數：只儲存在您的手機本機，開發者沒有副本。單則卦記可在 App 內刪除；解除安裝本 App（或在「設定 → 應用程式 → 謙卦 → 儲存空間 → 清除資料」）即全部刪除，無法復原。
  • 廣告 ID：可在 Android「設定 → 隱私權 → 廣告」（部分機型為「設定 → Google → 廣告」）選擇重設廣告 ID 或刪除廣告 ID。刪除後 AdMob 無法再將廣告資料與您的裝置連結；AdMob 已蒐集的資料由 Google 依其政策處理與保留。
  • 訂閱相關資料（RevenueCat）：請來信 lawrence124875@gmail.com，主旨註明「謙卦 資料刪除請求」，並附上 Google Play 訂單編號（GPA. 開頭，可在 Google Play「付款和訂閱 → 預算和記錄」或購買收據信中找到）。我們會在 30 天內向 RevenueCat 提出刪除該匿名使用者 ID 及其購買記錄的請求，完成後回信通知。刪除後如需恢復會員，可在 App 內按「恢復購買」重新建立。
  • Google Play 購買記錄：付款與訂單記錄由 Google 依 Google 隱私權政策及稅務、會計法規保留，開發者無法刪除；如需處理請洽 Google。
  • 使用統計與當機報告（Firebase）：不含可識別您身分的資訊，Analytics 事件資料保留 14 個月後自動刪除；如希望提前刪除與您裝置相關的資料，同樣可來信（同上）。

⚠️ 刪除資料或解除安裝不會取消訂閱。請先在 Google Play「付款和訂閱 → 訂閱」取消，以免繼續扣款。

Qiangua Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026
Developer: LC Lab (Lawrence Chang) · Contact: lawrence124875@gmail.com

Qiangua (“the App”) is a self-reflection tool based on the 64 hexagrams of the I Ching. The App requires no account or sign-in. All readings are written in advance and bundled in the App; no AI service is used, and nothing you write is sent to any server. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Journal: hexagrams you save, the question you wrote, your follow-up notes and review-reminder times are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them, and the App never uploads, backs up or shares them by itself. You can choose “Back up journal” in the journal list menu to save a backup file wherever you choose (for example your own cloud drive), and later bring it back with “Restore from backup”; the backup file never passes through the developer. Without a backup, uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them permanently.
  • Settings and counts, such as breathing session length and sound options, and the number of casts used today in the free version, are also stored only on your device.

Information collected to improve the App

  • Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): a few events that show how features are used — a reading was shown (casting method, hexagram number 1–64, whether there were changing lines), a journal entry was saved (whether a reminder was set), a breathing session was started or completed (hexagram number, minutes, whether silent guidance or binaural beats were on). Firebase Analytics also automatically collects general device and usage information (such as device model, OS version, App version, approximate region and session times) and uses device identifiers such as the app instance ID.
    Statistics never include your question, your notes or any text you type.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the App crashes, diagnostic information such as the stack trace, device model and OS version is collected to fix the problem. It contains no personal identity information or journal content.
  • Feature settings (Firebase Remote Config): the App periodically downloads feature switches. This uses the app instance ID and sends no personal data.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner at the bottom of the home screen and journal list, occasionally shows an interstitial ad when you return from a reading, and offers optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for one extra cast, or to save one more journal entry after reaching the free limit. No ads appear during breathing sessions or while you read. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), which may collect:

  • Device or other IDs: the Android advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID.
  • Approximate location: country- or city-level location derived from your IP address. The App does not use GPS or collect precise location.
  • App interactions: ad impressions, clicks and views, and usage information such as App launches.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: errors and performance data from the ads SDK itself (for example load times and latency).
  • Device and app information: device model, OS version, language and App version.

This data is used for advertising or marketing (serving ads, frequency capping and, with your consent, personalized ads), analytics (measuring ad performance), and fraud prevention, security and compliance (detecting invalid traffic and ad fraud). The developer only sees aggregated AdMob reports and cannot identify individual users.

  • Where consent is required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App first asks using Google’s consent form; you can change your choice at any time under “Ad privacy settings” in the menu at the top right of the home screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Sensitive ad categories such as alcohol and gambling are blocked, and ad content is limited to the PG rating.
  • Premium members see no ads, and no ads are loaded for them.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Subscription (Qiangua Premium)

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing; your payment details are handled by Google and are never visible to the developer. Subscription status is checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous user ID generated at random by the App (such as $RCAnonymousID:…, not linked to your Google account, name or email) and purchase history (plan, price and currency, purchase and expiry times, Google Play order ID and purchase token) and basic device and app information. This is used only to confirm membership, restore purchases and produce subscription statistics, and does not include your name or email address. Subscriptions renew automatically; cancel anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions, and you keep Premium until the end of the current period. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, rescheduling after reboot: used only for journal review reminders that you set yourself.
  • Foreground service (media playback), wake lock: used only so the breathing soundscape keeps playing with the screen off and shows pause/stop controls on the lock screen.
  • Internet, advertising ID: used for the Firebase services above, for ads in the free version, and for checking subscription status.
  • Google Play Billing: used only to purchase and restore subscriptions.

What the App does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No collection of names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • No selling or renting of user data.
  • No AI analysis of your questions or notes.

Data sharing

The statistics, crash and settings data above are processed by Google Firebase; ads by Google AdMob; subscriptions by Google Play and RevenueCat (see the sections above). All data is encrypted in transit. The App shares no data with any other third party, and never shares your journal or anything you type. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Children

The App is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Nature of the content

Readings are for self-reflection and entertainment only. They are not medical, psychological, legal, financial or other professional advice, and they do not predict the future.

Data deletion

This section applies to the Android app Qiangua (謙卦) (package com.lclab.qiangua), developed by LC Lab. The App has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Each type of data is deleted as follows:

  • Journal entries, questions, notes, settings and counts are stored only on your phone; the developer has no copy. Delete single entries in the App, or uninstall the App (or go to Settings → Apps → Qiangua → Storage → Clear data) to delete everything permanently.
  • Advertising ID: in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads), choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. After deletion AdMob can no longer link ad data to your device; data AdMob has already collected is handled and retained by Google under its policies.
  • Subscription data (RevenueCat): email lawrence124875@gmail.com with the subject “Qiangua data deletion request” and include your Google Play order ID (starting with GPA., found in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or in your receipt email). Within 30 days we will ask RevenueCat to delete that anonymous user ID and its purchase history, and reply to confirm. If you later want Premium back, tap “Restore purchases” in the App.
  • Google Play purchase records: payment and order records are retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and applicable tax and accounting laws; the developer cannot delete them. Please contact Google for these.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase) contain no information that identifies you; Analytics event data is deleted automatically after 14 months. To request earlier deletion of data associated with your device, email us as above.

⚠️ Deleting data or uninstalling the App does not cancel your subscription. Cancel first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to stop future charges.
