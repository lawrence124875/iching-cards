謙卦 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日
開發者：LC Lab（Lawrence Chang）　聯絡信箱：lawrence124875@gmail.com

「謙卦」（以下稱「本 App」）是以《易經》六十四卦為題材的自我探索與反思工具。本 App 不需要註冊或登入，解讀內容全部預先寫在 App 裡，不使用 AI 服務，也不會把您寫下的文字傳到任何伺服器。本政策說明本 App 會蒐集哪些資訊、如何使用，以及您擁有的權利。

只存在您手機裡的資料

  • 卦記：您保存的卦、寫下的「想問的事」、事後的回顧筆記，以及回顧提醒的時間，只儲存在您的裝置本機。開發者看不到，也不會上傳、備份或分享。解除安裝本 App 或清除 App 資料，這些內容會一併刪除且無法復原。
  • 設定與次數：例如呼吸音景的時長、鈴聲與雙耳節拍開關，以及免費版當日已起卦的次數，同樣只存在本機。

為改善 App 而蒐集的資訊

  • 匿名使用統計（Firebase Analytics）：記錄少數操作事件，以了解功能是否好用，例如：顯示了一次解讀（起卦方式、卦序 1–64、是否有變爻）、保存了一則卦記（是否設定提醒）、開始或完成一次呼吸練習（卦序、分鐘數、是否開啟無聲引導或雙耳節拍）。Firebase Analytics 也會自動收集一般的裝置與使用資訊（例如裝置型號、系統版本、App 版本、大致地區、使用時段），並使用 App 實例識別碼等裝置識別碼。
    統計絕不包含您寫下的「想問的事」、回顧筆記或任何您輸入的文字。
  • 當機報告（Firebase Crashlytics）：App 發生當機時，蒐集錯誤堆疊、裝置型號、系統版本等診斷資訊，用於修正問題，不包含您的個人身分資訊或卦記內容。
  • 功能設定（Firebase Remote Config）：App 會定期下載功能開關設定（例如暫時關閉某項功能），此過程會使用 App 實例識別碼，不傳送您的個人資料。

廣告（免費版）

免費版會在首頁與卦記列表底部顯示橫幅廣告、看完解讀回到起卦頁時偶爾顯示插頁廣告，並提供您自行選擇觀看的獎勵廣告（看完可多起一卦，或在卦記達免費上限時多存一則）。呼吸練習與閱讀解讀時不會跳出廣告。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，AdMob 可能蒐集並使用：裝置廣告識別碼、IP 位址與由此推得的大致地區、裝置與 App 資訊、廣告互動紀錄，用於投放、計算成效、限制頻率與防止詐欺；在您同意的情況下也可能用於個人化廣告。

  • 在需要取得同意的地區（例如歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士），App 會先以 Google 的同意表單詢問您，之後可從首頁右上角選單的「廣告隱私設定」隨時更改。
  • 您可在裝置系統設定中重設或刪除廣告識別碼，或選擇停用個人化廣告。
  • 本 App 在 AdMob 封鎖酒精、賭博等敏感類別的廣告，廣告內容分級上限為「家長指導（PG）」。
  • 訂閱會員不顯示任何廣告，也不會載入廣告。

AdMob 的資料處理方式請見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

訂閱（謙卦會員）

月訂與年訂經由 Google Play 結帳完成，付款資料（信用卡等）由 Google 處理，開發者看不到。訂閱狀態透過 RevenueCat 服務確認：RevenueCat 會收到一組隨機產生的匿名使用者 ID、購買紀錄（方案、購買與到期時間、交易編號）以及裝置與 App 基本資訊，只用於確認您是否為會員、恢復購買與訂閱統計，不含您的姓名或電子郵件。訂閱會自動續訂，可隨時在 Google Play 的「付款和訂閱」中取消，取消後會員資格保留到本期結束。RevenueCat 的資料處理方式請見 RevenueCat 隱私權政策。

App 使用的權限

  • 通知、準時鬧鐘、開機後重新排程：只用於您自行設定的「卦記回顧提醒」，在您選定的日子提醒您回來看這一卦。
  • 前景服務（媒體播放）、保持喚醒：只用於呼吸音景，讓您關閉螢幕或切到其他 App 時音景能繼續播放，並在鎖定畫面顯示暫停、停止鍵。
  • 網路、廣告識別碼：用於上述 Firebase 服務、顯示廣告（免費版）與確認訂閱狀態。
  • Google Play 結帳：只用於購買與恢復訂閱。

本 App 不會做的事

  • 不要求註冊帳號或登入。
  • 不蒐集姓名、電話、電子郵件、地址、相片、聯絡人或精確位置。
  • 不出售或出租任何使用者資料。
  • 不使用 AI 服務分析您的提問或筆記。

資料分享

上述統計、當機與設定資料由 Google 的 Firebase 服務處理；廣告由 Google AdMob 處理；訂閱由 Google Play 與 RevenueCat 處理（見上方各段）。傳輸過程皆經過加密。除此之外，本 App 不與任何第三方分享資料，也絕不分享您的卦記與輸入的文字。Firebase 的資料處理方式請見 Firebase 隱私權與安全性說明。

兒童隱私

本 App 並非為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意向兒童蒐集個人資訊。

內容性質

本 App 的卦象解讀僅供自我探索與娛樂參考，不構成任何醫療、心理、法律、財務或其他專業建議，也不保證預測結果。

如何刪除您的資料

  • 卦記與設定：只在您的裝置上。刪除單則卦記可在 App 內操作；在系統設定中清除本 App 的資料或解除安裝，即全部刪除。
  • 使用統計與當機報告：不含可識別您身分的資訊，依 Google Firebase 的保留政策處理（Analytics 事件資料保留期限為 14 個月）。若您希望刪除與您裝置相關的統計資料，請來信 lawrence124875@gmail.com，主旨註明「謙卦 資料刪除請求」，我們會盡快處理並回覆。
  • 廣告資料：由 Google 依其政策處理；重設或刪除裝置廣告識別碼即可切斷與先前廣告資料的關聯。
  • 訂閱紀錄：可來信（同上）要求刪除 RevenueCat 中與您匿名 ID 相關的資料；Google Play 的交易紀錄依 Google 政策保存。刪除資料不會自動取消訂閱，請先在 Google Play 取消。

Qiangua Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026
Developer: LC Lab (Lawrence Chang) · Contact: lawrence124875@gmail.com

Qiangua (“the App”) is a self-reflection tool based on the 64 hexagrams of the I Ching. The App requires no account or sign-in. All readings are written in advance and bundled in the App; no AI service is used, and nothing you write is sent to any server. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Journal: hexagrams you save, the question you wrote, your follow-up notes and review-reminder times are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them, and they are never uploaded, backed up or shared. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them permanently.
  • Settings and counts, such as breathing session length and sound options, and the number of casts used today in the free version, are also stored only on your device.

Information collected to improve the App

  • Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): a few events that show how features are used — a reading was shown (casting method, hexagram number 1–64, whether there were changing lines), a journal entry was saved (whether a reminder was set), a breathing session was started or completed (hexagram number, minutes, whether silent guidance or binaural beats were on). Firebase Analytics also automatically collects general device and usage information (such as device model, OS version, App version, approximate region and session times) and uses device identifiers such as the app instance ID.
    Statistics never include your question, your notes or any text you type.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the App crashes, diagnostic information such as the stack trace, device model and OS version is collected to fix the problem. It contains no personal identity information or journal content.
  • Feature settings (Firebase Remote Config): the App periodically downloads feature switches. This uses the app instance ID and sends no personal data.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner at the bottom of the home screen and journal list, occasionally shows an interstitial ad when you return from a reading, and offers optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for one extra cast, or to save one more journal entry after reaching the free limit. No ads appear during breathing sessions or while you read. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use the device advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions, for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention, and, with your consent, for personalized ads.

  • Where consent is required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App first asks using Google’s consent form; you can change your choice at any time under “Ad privacy settings” in the menu at the top right of the home screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Sensitive ad categories such as alcohol and gambling are blocked, and ad content is limited to the PG rating.
  • Premium members see no ads, and no ads are loaded for them.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Subscription (Qiangua Premium)

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing; your payment details are handled by Google and are never visible to the developer. Subscription status is checked through RevenueCat, which receives a randomly generated anonymous user ID, purchase records (plan, purchase and expiry times, transaction ID) and basic device and app information. This is used only to confirm membership, restore purchases and produce subscription statistics, and does not include your name or email address. Subscriptions renew automatically; cancel anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions, and you keep Premium until the end of the current period. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, rescheduling after reboot: used only for journal review reminders that you set yourself.
  • Foreground service (media playback), wake lock: used only so the breathing soundscape keeps playing with the screen off and shows pause/stop controls on the lock screen.
  • Internet, advertising ID: used for the Firebase services above, for ads in the free version, and for checking subscription status.
  • Google Play Billing: used only to purchase and restore subscriptions.

What the App does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No collection of names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • No selling or renting of user data.
  • No AI analysis of your questions or notes.

Data sharing

The statistics, crash and settings data above are processed by Google Firebase; ads by Google AdMob; subscriptions by Google Play and RevenueCat (see the sections above). All data is encrypted in transit. The App shares no data with any other third party, and never shares your journal or anything you type. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Children

The App is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Nature of the content

Readings are for self-reflection and entertainment only. They are not medical, psychological, legal, financial or other professional advice, and they do not predict the future.

How to delete your data

  • Journal and settings exist only on your device. Delete single entries in the App, or clear the App’s data / uninstall it to delete everything.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports contain no information that identifies you and are retained under Google Firebase policies (Analytics event data: 14 months). To request deletion of statistics associated with your device, email lawrence124875@gmail.com with the subject “Qiangua data deletion request”.
  • Ad data is handled by Google under its policies; resetting or deleting your advertising ID breaks the link to earlier ad data.
  • Subscription records: you can email us (as above) to delete RevenueCat data linked to your anonymous ID; Google Play keeps transaction records under its own policies. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription; please cancel in Google Play first.
